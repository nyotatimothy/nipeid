📱 Nipe ID - Antler Pitch Script

5-Minute Spoken Presentation Guide

Complete Speaker Notes & Q&A Preparation

Opening Hook (30 seconds)
"Imagine losing your National ID tomorrow." In Kenya, this happens to over 1,000 people every single day. Without that small plastic card, you can't open a bank account, get a job, or even buy a SIM card. You're essentially locked out of modern life.
[Brief pause for impact]
The current solution? Visit multiple government offices, wait 3-6 months, and pay over 2,000 shillings. For millions of Kenyans, especially in rural areas, this isn't just inconvenient—it's impossible.
[Pause]
We're solving this with Nipe ID—turning document recovery from months to minutes.
The Problem (45 seconds)
Kenya has 50 million citizens who depend on physical documents for everything. When these documents are lost—and 365,000 are lost annually—people face a bureaucratic nightmare.
There's no centralized system to track found documents. No way for finders to connect with owners. No digital infrastructure supporting recovery.
This isn't just a Kenyan problem. Across East Africa, 200 million people face identical challenges. Globally, 2.5 billion people in emerging markets struggle with document recovery.
The cost? $6.8 million annually in Kenya alone—lost productivity, recovery fees, and opportunity costs.
Our Solution (60 seconds)
Nipe ID is a digital platform that transforms document recovery. Here's how it works:
Someone finds a lost ID and takes it to any participating kiosk—M-Pesa agents, shops, government offices. The kiosk agent uploads the document to our secure platform.
Our AI-powered system immediately searches for potential owners using name matching and location algorithms. Within minutes, we send SMS notifications to matched individuals—no smartphone required.
The rightful owner verifies their identity through multi-factor authentication, then collects their document at the nearest kiosk. What used to take months now takes minutes.
We've built this entire infrastructure: SMS integration with Africa's Talking, M-Pesa payment systems, kiosk management dashboards, and secure document handling—all optimized for Kenya's unique digital ecosystem.
Market & Business Model (60 seconds)
The immediate market is massive. Kenya's 50 million citizens, 15,000 potential kiosk partners, and 365,000 annual document losses create a $6.8 million market.
Our business model scales beautifully. We charge 200-500 shillings per successful recovery—10-25% of the traditional cost. Kiosk partners keep 60%, we take 40%.
At just 1,000 recoveries per month, we generate 200,000-500,000 shillings in revenue. Our unit economics are strong: 100 shilling customer acquisition costs, 350 shilling average recovery value, and 70% gross margins.
But this is just phase one. We'll expand to subscription models for kiosks, enterprise partnerships with government, and cross-border expansion across East Africa.
Traction & Why Now (45 seconds)
We're not just an idea—we're execution. Our full-stack platform is built and deployed. SMS infrastructure is production-ready. We have kiosk management systems, admin dashboards, and mobile-responsive design.
The timing is perfect. Kenya's Digital Economy Blueprint prioritizes identity solutions. Mobile money adoption hit 96%. SMS coverage reaches 99% of the population. Post-COVID acceleration toward digital services creates unprecedented demand.
We're in partnership discussions with three major kiosk chains and have government stakeholder meetings scheduled. Beta testing with five kiosks launches Q1 2025.

🤔 Q&A Preparation

Expected Questions & Polished Responses

Q: "What about competition from government solutions?"
A: "We're not competing with government—we're partnering. Our platform integrates with existing infrastructure and can even be white-labeled for government use. We make the system more efficient, not replace it. Think of us as the digital layer that enhances what's already there."
Q: "How do you ensure document security and prevent fraud?"
A: "Multi-layer security: encrypted uploads, multi-factor authentication, kiosk partner verification, SMS confirmation, and blockchain-ready audit trails. Plus, we're working with regulatory experts to exceed compliance requirements. Security isn't an afterthought—it's built into every layer."
Q: "What's your customer acquisition strategy?"
A: "Three-pronged approach: SMS marketing directly to recent ID applicants through government partnerships, kiosk network effects creating viral growth, and word-of-mouth in communities. Our low CAC of 100 shillings makes this highly scalable. The product essentially markets itself when it works."
Q: "How quickly can you expand regionally?"
A: "The infrastructure is designed for rapid expansion. Similar document systems, established SMS/mobile money networks, and our modular architecture mean we can launch in Uganda or Tanzania within 6 months of proven Kenya traction. The playbook is replicable."
Q: "What's your path to profitability?"
A: "Break-even at 1,500 recoveries per month, which we project to hit by month 8-10. With 70% gross margins and low customer acquisition costs, profitability scales quickly. Government contracts could accelerate this significantly."
Q: "How do you handle different document types and verification?"
A: "We start with National IDs—the highest volume and most critical. Our verification system is designed to expand to passports, licenses, certificates. Each document type has specific verification workflows, but the core infrastructure supports all."

🎯 Presentation Tips

Delivery Guidelines

  • Pace: Speak slowly and clearly—complex topic needs time to absorb
  • Energy: High energy on problem and solution, calm confidence on execution
  • Eye contact: Engage each team member, don't just focus on lead
  • Gestures: Use hands to emphasize scale (50 million, months to minutes)
  • Pauses: Strategic pauses after key statistics for impact

Key Moments

  • Opening hook: Personal, relatable problem everyone understands
  • Solution demo: If possible, show actual platform screenshots
  • Market size: Emphasize both Kenya scale and global opportunity
  • Traction proof: "Production-ready" carries weight—emphasize this
  • Closing ask: Direct, confident request for partnership
