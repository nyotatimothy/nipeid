🚨 The Problem: Lost Documents Lock Out Millions

"Every day in Kenya, over 1,000 people lose their National ID. For many, this isn't just an inconvenience—it's a barrier to accessing basic services, employment, and dignity."
50M+ Kenyans depend on physical documents
365K+ Documents lost annually
3-6 months Current recovery time

The Current Reality

  • Lost documents = Lost opportunities: Can't open bank accounts, get jobs, access healthcare
  • Bureaucratic nightmare: Multiple government visits, lengthy processes
  • High costs: KES 2,000+ in fees, transport, and lost productivity
  • No centralized system: No way to track or recover found documents
  • Rural disadvantage: Limited access to government offices

Economic Impact

The cost extends far beyond individual inconvenience. Kenya loses approximately $6.8 million annually in direct recovery costs, with broader economic impacts reaching $15M annually including lost productivity and opportunity costs. This affects:

  • Financial inclusion (can't access banking services)
  • Employment opportunities (can't prove identity)
  • Healthcare access (limited service eligibility)
  • Digital services (can't register for mobile money, internet services)
