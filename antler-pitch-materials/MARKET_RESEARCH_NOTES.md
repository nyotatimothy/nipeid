**Our calculations used $73M which may need adjustment - this seems high. Better estimate: $6.7M-$15M annually**

### Direct Competitors:
**Currently: None identified**
- No existing digital document recovery platforms

### Indirect Competitors:
- **Government replacement services** (slow, expensive)
- **Physical bulletin boards** (limited reach)

### Competitive Advantages:
